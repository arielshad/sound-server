## Sound of movement

A whoosh is the sound of something passing quickly: air moved by a swinging object, or a burst of filtered noise that rises and falls. In music it fills the moment before a section changes; in video it sells a cut or a title. Most are under two seconds long and peak in the middle.

## Choosing and placing

Time the loudest point of the whoosh to land exactly on the cut or downbeat, not its start. For music, a high-pass keeps it out of the bass, and a tempo-synced delay can stretch it into a transition. Recorded whooshes (sticks, cloth, ropes swung past a microphone) sound natural; synthesised noise sweeps sound cleaner and more electronic.
