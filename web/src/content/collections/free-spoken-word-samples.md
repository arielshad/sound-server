## Words as texture

A spoken line can set the mood of a track before a note plays: a narrator, a radio announcer, a fragment of an old speech. Ambient, hip hop, techno and lo-fi producers all use spoken samples as intros, breakdowns and hooks. This collection gathers free recordings of speech: readings, field recordings of announcements, public-domain archive material and voice lines recorded for reuse.

## Rights and reuse

Spoken samples are where producers most often get into trouble, because the most tempting lines come from films, TV and interviews. Those need clearance. Public-domain archives, such as the many government and historic recordings on Wikimedia Commons and the Internet Archive, are a better source, but check each one: the Internet Archive relies on what uploaders state, so Sound Server never rates those better than "check the terms".

When a recording needs a credit, the detail view gives you the line to paste.
