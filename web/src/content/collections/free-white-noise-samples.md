## The most useful sound with no pitch

Noise contains every frequency at once, which makes it a raw material for other sounds. Filter it and sweep the cutoff up, and you have a riser. Shape it with a short envelope, and it becomes a snare layer or a hi-hat. Leave it steady, and it masks distractions, which is why noise tracks are popular for sleep and focus.

## Colours of noise

White noise is the brightest; pink and brown noise put more energy into the low end. Producers usually reach for white noise for risers and snares and pink noise for reference mixing, since its spectrum is closer to how music is balanced. Synth-generated noise is easy to make yourself, but recorded noise sources such as tape hiss, radio static and air conditioners have character that a generator lacks.
