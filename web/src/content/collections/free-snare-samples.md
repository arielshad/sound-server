## What to listen for

A snare has three parts: the stick attack, the tone of the drum head, and the rattle of the snare wires. Tight, dry snares suit funk, boom bap and modern pop; roomy snares with a long tail suit rock and ballads; bright, compressed drum machine snares suit house and electro.

Tune the snare to your track. Pitching a sample up a semitone or two often brings it forward without extra EQ, while pitching down makes it fatter and slower. Watch the tail: on fast tempos a long ring will overlap the next hit.
