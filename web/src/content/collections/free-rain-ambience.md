## Rain in music

Rain is the most used ambience in lo-fi and chill music, and it works well under intros, outros and spoken word. A steady, mid-intensity rain fills space without stealing attention; a downpour or thunder makes a statement. Field recordings capture the space they were made in, so rain on a tin roof, a car windscreen or leaves each sound distinct.

Keep ambience low and filter the extremes: roll off some low end to avoid rumble and some top end if it competes with hi-hats. Stereo recordings give width that a mono loop can't.

## Sources

Most free rain recordings come from field recordists on Freesound and Wikimedia Commons, many under CC0 or CC BY. Broadcaster archives also have rain, but often under non-commercial terms, which the badge marks in red.
