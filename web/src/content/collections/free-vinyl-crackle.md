## Texture for lo-fi and beyond

Vinyl crackle is the quiet bed of pops and hiss that makes a beat sound like it was lifted from an old record. It glues samples from different sources together and fills the silence between notes. Lo-fi hip hop, chillhop and ambient lean on it, and a touch of it can soften a bright modern production too.

Look for long takes that loop cleanly, so the pattern of pops doesn't repeat every bar. Crackle recorded from the run-out groove of a record contains no music, which keeps it free of copyright problems.

## Licences

Most crackle in archives is CC0 or CC BY, recorded by the uploader. If a sample includes music from the record itself, treat it with care: the badge shows whether the licence was verified by the source.
